// 01 — About
Who I am

I'm Bhaskar, a robotics engineer based in Munich, Germany. Having recently completed my Master's degree at TUM, my thesis focused on training RL agents to push objects along complex trajectories using a Franka Panda arm in MuJoCo simulation.

My background spans the full mechatronics stack: from embedded PCB design and industrial hardware to SLAM, computer vision, and deep reinforcement learning. I'm equally passionate about securing the systems I build — bridging OT and IT security in an era of connected automation.

Outside the lab, I'm always tinkering — whether it's a custom NFC-powered sensor board or debugging Bluetooth on a triple-monitor Linux setup.
