// 01 — About
Who I am

I'm Bhaskar, a robotics engineer based in Munich, Germany. Having recently completed my Master's degree at TUM, my thesis focused on training RL agents to push objects along complex trajectories using a Franka Panda arm in MuJoCo simulation.

My background spans the full mechatronics stack: from embedded PCB design and industrial hardware to SLAM, computer vision, and deep reinforcement learning. I'm equally passionate about securing the systems I build — bridging OT and IT security in an era of connected automation.

Outside the lab, I'm always tinkering — whether it's a custom NFC-powered sensor board or debugging Bluetooth on a triple-monitor Linux setup.

// 03 — Projects
Selected Work
🤖

RL-Based Robotic Push Control

Master's thesis: PPO-trained Franka Panda agent that pushes objects along straight, Bézier, and S-curve trajectories in MuJoCo. Custom reward shaping with progress tracking, proximity gating, and orientation alignment.

PythonMuJoCoPPOGymnasium
📡

NFC Thermocouple Sensor PCB

Designed a battery-free NFC sensor board in KiCad for temperature data transmission at FRM II. Uses TI RF430FRL152H with OPA333 op-amp and NFC energy harvesting — no external power needed.

KiCadNFCEmbeddedAnalog
🦾

Wire Loop Navigation — KUKA LBR iiwa

Reactive force-feedback control for navigating a wire loop game with a 7-DOF KUKA arm. Explored geometric constraints of step-size vs. arc-length on sharp curves.

ROSC++Force ControlKUKA
🚗

TAS-Car Autonomous Vehicle

Contributed to an autonomous driving platform integrating SLAM, perception, and path planning in a real-world research vehicle at TUM.
